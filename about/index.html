Alongside Korek I was Account Manager at Multimedia Agency, Korek's own agency: around ten client projects, both the client side and the studio side, at 95% on-time completion. The fix that made the difference was the same one I used at Hitex: start earlier, take a proper brief, and bring options to the approver instead of one version.
