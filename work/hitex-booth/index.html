The Korek stand at Hitex

Erbil's technology exhibition — 2022, 2023, 2024

04 — 08Korek · 2022–2024
stand · diamond tier
SpecifyDesignShip

Hitex is Iraq's biggest technology exhibition, held in Erbil: 150+ exhibitors from more than 20 countries, 50,000+ visitors by the fair's own count. Korek sponsors it at diamond level, the top tier, and has done for five years running.

One stand had to do three jobs at once — show Korek as the technology leader in Kurdistan, sign people up on the spot, and host the government and business visits. Those usually need three different spaces.

What I did

The stand itself, not the advertising around it. The concept was mine. An outside company built it each year and I supervised that build, then ran the stand on site during the fair.

Each year it did something different. A live draw for visitors in 2022. A VR experience in 2023 — that one was my idea. By 2024 it was the stop the Prime Minister, Masrour Barzani, was walked through, and I was on the stand running it that day.

The hard part

Getting it built in time. The first design never matched what management wanted, so it went back and forth until they were happy — and by then production was late. We were finishing the stand the night before or the morning of, two or three hours before visitors came in.

The problem was not how fast I worked, it was how the decision was being made. So I changed two things: I started the design earlier each year, and I stopped bringing one version to the approval. I brought options upfront instead.

Later years were finished well ahead of opening.

Results

Korek held a diamond-tier stand at Iraq's biggest technology exhibition — a fair of 150+ exhibitors from 21 countries. That scale is the fair's, not the stand's; I have no visitor or sign-up numbers of my own.
