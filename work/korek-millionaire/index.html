Korek Millionaire & Korek Treasure

Korek Telecom's prize-draw competitions — 2021 to 2025

01 — 08Korek · 2021–2025
16:9 · live · monthly
SpecifyDesignShip

Korek ran two SMS competitions side by side: Korek Millionaire on 377 and Korek Treasure on 3100. Subscribers answered questions by text to collect points, and every season closed with a draw for something big — a car, cash, a house.

I made the marketing around both, for four to five years.

What I did

Visual posts, video posts and TV ads. Some of the ads were my idea, some were fully mine. Agencies shot several of them, to my concept and under my supervision.

The part I owned outright was the draws. I shot and edited them myself, in studio and on green screen, and they went out live on Korek's own pages, with no second take. The small draws ran monthly, the big ones every six months. An ad can slip a week. A live draw cannot.

The hard part

Same game, same mechanic, same audience, season after season. Keeping it from feeling like a repeat was the whole problem. I changed two things each season: the story and the faces. A cinematic treatment one season, a singer fronting the next, a winner's reveal after that.

Management judged the campaign on one number — how many people played.

Results

I have no figures of my own here. What Korek published is public and dated: a 50 million IQD winner announced in October 2025, a 3 million IQD prize in 2022, and a prize ladder running from a Renault and a Mini Cooper up to a Land Cruiser and a house. Korek posted about the two competitions 43 times between April 2021 and October 2025.

No draw footage survived, so this one carries no clip.
