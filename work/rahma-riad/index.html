Rahma Riad — "اني الأقوى"

A brand film series for Korek Telecom, 2022

05 — 08Korek · 2022
film · 3 parts
SpecifyDesignShip

Iraq has three mobile operators, and all three were signing celebrities. Korek's answer was a film series with Rahma Riad — one of the country's biggest singers.

The brief was not to sell a package. It was to make Korek feel stronger. Rahma Riad is loved, and Korek wanted to borrow that affection rather than buy attention.

My part

I was the brand guardian on it. An agency directed and shot; my job was to make sure Korek survived the shoot and the edit — the colours, the logo, the styling, all of it staying on brand.

That meant being on set with them, and then going through the cuts and giving notes. Not a sign-off at the end.

The work

One film, released in parts: the full film first, a shorter cut from it, then a second part built around Alex, a major influencer and Rahma Riad's husband. A separate Rahma Riad job followed later for Korek's Blue loyalty programme.

Results

These are the song's numbers, not mine — I was the brand guardian on a film that performed like this. Korek publicly announced that it hit number one on YouTube trending in Iraq within hours of release, and within days had passed 1.5 million views across YouTube and Facebook plus 700,000 on Instagram.

The pieces

4 of the 68 pieces on Korek's own pages that I made or ran, as they were published.
