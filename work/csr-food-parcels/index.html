Ramadan food parcels

Korek Telecom's CSR campaign, 2021 and 2023

06 — 08Korek · 2021–2023
box · 5,500 families
SpecifyDesignShip

Every Ramadan Korek ran a food-parcel campaign with the Barzani Charity Foundation, and later with the Red Crescent as well. Families received a box of food; the campaign was how Korek showed up as part of the community rather than as a phone company.

What I did

Four jobs on one campaign.

The food box itself was my idea and my design — the physical package families opened. I supervised its production. I made every design used across the campaign. And I cut the film.

My part ended when the boxes and the film were finished; the footage of the handover is other people's, and I edited it.

Results

Korek announced that the boxes went to more than 5,500 families across the Kurdistan Region in Ramadan 2021, with the Barzani Charity Foundation. That is Korek's reach, not mine. My part of it is the box in their hands and the film that told the story.
