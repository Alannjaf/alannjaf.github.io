Korek's student internship programme

Korek Telecom, 2023 to 2026

02 — 08Korek · 2023–2026
4 cities · 200 a year
SpecifyDesignShip

Every summer Korek took university students in and gave them a real working environment. It ran nationally — Erbil, Baghdad, Sulaymaniyah and Duhok — and roughly 200 students came through it each year.

Korek's teams trained them. I owned everything around it.

What I did

I supervised the programme's delivery. I sourced the gifts the students were given. I made every piece of material the programme used. I directed the photographer and the videographer, and I cut the films myself.

That is the whole visible side of it — what the students got in their hands and what the country saw of it.

The work

Three films across the 2023 season: the Erbil universities, the trainees telling their own success stories, and the closing trip to Baghdad for the graduation.

Results

Roughly 200 students each year, 2023 to 2026.

3 of the 68 pieces on Korek's own pages that I made or ran, as they were published.
