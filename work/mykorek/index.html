MyKorek

Korek Telecom's self-service app — the 2024 redesign

03 — 08Korek · 2024
430 × 932 · 58 screens
SpecifyDesignShip

Korek is one of Iraq's largest mobile operators. MyKorek is where its customers check their balance, buy offers, top up, and join the Blue loyalty programme.

In June 2024 the app relaunched. I designed it.

What I designed

The screens and the layout, and the graphics inside them — 58 phone screens in Figma, built as a component system rather than loose artwork, with a full set of design options for the offers page. The home screen holds the balance header, the Top Offers carousel, and the menu into Controls, Plans, Cashback, Rewards and Draw.

The app launched with this design.

The work around it

I also made MyKorek's marketing, across both generations of the app: two videos and two designs. The 2023 pieces advertise the app as it stood then. The June 2024 video announces the one I had designed — the promo and the product were the same job.

I animated the September 2023 video myself, and one of the 2023 designs ran as a billboard, not only on social. No agency on any of them.

Results

I redesigned 58 screens. Before that release the app was rated 3.0 on Google Play with 100K+ installs. Today it is 4.5 with 1M+ installs from 18.7K reviews, and 4.6 on the App Store from 8,491 ratings.

The pieces

4 of the 68 pieces on Korek's own pages that I made or ran, as they were published.
