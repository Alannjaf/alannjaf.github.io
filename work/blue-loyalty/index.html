Blue

Korek Telecom's loyalty programme — 2022 to 2026

07 — 08Korek · 2022–2026
1:1 · 29 pieces
SpecifyDesignShip

Blue is Korek's loyalty programme: every dinar you spend earns points, and points buy rewards — minutes, internet, coupons, discounts. It is free to join, through the MyKorek app or by texting 1 to 3500. Later years added draws, including a monthly million-dinar prize.

I made Blue's marketing material, for four years — from 2022 until I left Korek in March 2026.

What I did

Concept and design, both mine, on every piece.

On one of them I took the studio photograph myself. The 2022 anniversary video I shot and directed myself, on greenscreen.

No agency on any of it — which is not true of most of the other work I did at Korek.

The scale of it

Blue is the longest-running thing I worked on at Korek. There are 117 Blue posts on Korek's page; 29 of them are mine.

A few were published after I had already left, because I had prepared them before I went.

Results

Korek does not publish Blue's numbers, so I do not use any. What I can show is four years of the work itself.

The pieces

9 of the 68 pieces on Korek's own pages that I made or ran, as they were published.
